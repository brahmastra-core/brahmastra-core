# LEGAL & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE AUDIT
## BRAHMASTRA SOVEREIGN PROTOCOL ARCHITECTURE
**Frameworks Evaluated**: Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) & EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)  
**Jurisdiction Baseline**: Canton of Zug, Switzerland  
**Date of Ratification**: November 6, 2026  

---

### 1. DATA MINIMIZATION & ZERO-LOGGING MANDATE
* **GDPR Article 5(1)(c) Compliance**: The Brahmastra mixnet relay nodes operate strictly without logging IP addresses, transaction timestamps, or user metadata.
* **Deterministic Forward Secrecy**: Ephemeral post-quantum lattice keys are erased immediately after onion packet unwrapping, guaranteeing that no recoverable personal identifiable information (PII) is stored at rest.

### 2. PRIVACY BY DESIGN & DEFAULT
* **GDPR Article 25 & Swiss FADP Article 7**: Sovereign encryption is activated by default at the client terminal before entering the decentralized transport mesh.
* **Non-Custodial Topology**: No centralized server, directory, or foundation trustee maintains decryption escrow capabilities.

### 3. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS & SOVEREIGN EXCLUSION
* **GDPR Chapter V & Swiss FADP Chapter 2**: Because packets are cryptographically transformed at every mix-node hop using Sphinx multi-layer encapsulation, cross-border packets constitute zero readable export data.
* **Jurisdictional Immunity**: Mix nodes route encrypted payloads without possessing legal ownership or intermediate awareness of cleartext contents.

### 4. COMPLIANCE DETERMINATION & RISK CERTIFICATION
The Brahmastra Core Protocol architecture fulfills all statutory obligations under current Swiss and European open-source digital privacy jurisprudence. It qualifies as a resilient public utility protocol designed strictly for cryptographic data integrity and self-sovereign communications.
