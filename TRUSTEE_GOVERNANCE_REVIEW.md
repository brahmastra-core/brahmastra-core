# TRUSTEE GOVERNANCE & FIDUCIARY REVIEW
## BRAHMASTRA DHARMA TRUST (INDIA)

- **Fiduciary Framework**: Indian Trusts Act & Public Charitable Trust Guidelines
- **Trustee Duties & Controls**:
  1. Founder / Managing Trustee holds permanent architectural and operational oversight.
  2. Absolute segregation between protocol engineering and humanitarian relief disbursements.
  3. All transactions logged on-chain and cross-referenced with statutory audits.
- **Audit Mandate**: Annual statutory audits filed with competent fiscal authorities.
