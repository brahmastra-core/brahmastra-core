# STATUTORY COMPLIANCE REVIEW: FOREIGN CONTRIBUTION REGULATION ACT (FCRA)
## BRAHMASTRA DHARMA TRUST (INDIA)

- **Governing Law**: Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 (read with FCRA Amendment Act, 2020)
- **Oversight Authority**: Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India
- **Core Statutory Compliance Parameters**:
  1. Mandatory Primary Account: Sole routing of overseas institutional grants via State Bank of India (SBI), New Delhi Main Branch.
  2. Administrative Overhead Limit: Operational and administrative expenditures strictly constrained within the statutory 20% ceiling.
  3. Absolute Prohibition on Sub-Granting: Inward humanitarian funding utilized exclusively by Brahmastra Dharma Trust without diversion or third-party NGO onward transfers.
  4. Non-Speculative Welfare Focus: Complete exclusion from speculative investments, political associations, or commercial activity.
- **Audit Finding**: Charter covenants completely aligned with MHA statutory frameworks.
