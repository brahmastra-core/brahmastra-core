# DECLARATION OF INTERNATIONAL JURISDICTIONAL NEUTRALITY
## BRAHMASTRA SOVEREIGN PROTOCOL & FOUNDATION

1. **JURISDICTIONAL ANCHOR**:
   The Brahmastra Sovereign Foundation is incorporated under the neutral jurisdiction of the Canton of Zug, Switzerland, pursuant to Articles 60 et seq. of the Swiss Civil Code (ZGB).

2. **GLOBAL PUBLIC UTILITY**:
   The cryptographic primitives, routing architecture, and zero-trust mechanisms of the Brahmastra Protocol serve exclusively as a non-military, non-political, decentralized public good.

3. **INDEPENDENT GOVERNANCE**:
   No single nation-state, regulatory bloc, or centralized private corporation holds administrative or cryptographic backdoors into the network.

4. **PERPETUAL HUMANITARIAN COVENANT**:
   Eighty percent (80%) of surplus protocol micro-fees remain irrevocably designated for global public healthcare, education, and agrarian disaster relief.
