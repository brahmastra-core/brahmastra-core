# CONSTITUTION & STATUTES OF THE BRAHMASTRA SOVEREIGN FOUNDATION
# (Swiss Non-Profit Association Framework - Canton of Zug)

## ARTICLE 1: NAME, PURPOSE, AND CORE MANDATE
1.1. Under the name "Brahmastra Sovereign Foundation", a non-profit association is established pursuant to Articles 60 et seq. of the Swiss Civil Code (ZGB).
1.2. The primary mission is the perpetual stewardship of the Brahmastra post-quantum zero-trust communication protocol and the unalterable execution of humanitarian public welfare.

## ARTICLE 2: THE 80/20 IRREVOCABLE WELFARE & SUSTAINABILITY COVENANT
2.1. **80% Public Dharma Allocation**: Exactly eighty percent (80%) of all protocol micro-fees, institutional network royalties, and surplus foundation revenues are perpetually, irrevocably locked for public good and direct benefit distribution:
    - **Free Healthcare**: Direct settlement of critical surgeries, emergency trauma, and cancer therapies for underprivileged families.
    - **Free Education**: Sponsorship of educational fees, technical training, and essentials for impoverished students.
    - **Agrarian & Disaster Relief**: Direct emergency financial relief for smallholder farmers suffering crop loss from droughts, floods, or natural calamities.
2.2. **20% Protocol Sustainability & Founder Equity**: The remaining twenty percent (20%) is allocated exclusively for core operational infrastructure, network auditing, node hosting, and the executive stipend, legal protection, and perpetual compensation of the Founding Architect.

## ARTICLE 3: FOUNDER SOVEREIGNTY & BDFL TECHNICAL AUTHORITY
3.1. The Lead Architect and Original Creator of the Brahmastra Protocol is recognized as the **Benevolent Dictator for Life (BDFL) and Founding Trustee**.
3.2. **Exclusive Architectural Stewardship**: The Founding Trustee retains absolute, non-appealable veto power and master authorization over all cryptographic primitives, consensus logic, protocol code merges, and network upgrades.
3.3. **Irrevocable Appointment**: The Founding Trustee's tenure is lifetime, unchallengeable, and protected against hostile council replacement or administrative dilution.

## ARTICLE 4: FINANCIAL TREASURY & DISBURSEMENT CONTROLS
4.1. All multi-signature vaults, institutional bank accounts, and grant distributions strictly require the cryptographic signature and explicit authorization of the Founding Trustee.

## ARTICLE 5: ADOPTION AND RATIFICATION
5.1. These updated Statutes are formally ratified into the canonical protocol architecture on this 6th day of November, 2026.
