# CONSTITUTION & STATUTES OF THE BRAHMASTRA SOVEREIGN FOUNDATION
# (Swiss Non-Profit Association Framework - Canton of Zug)

## ARTICLE 1: NAME, PURPOSE, AND LEGAL FORM
1.1. Under the name "Brahmastra Sovereign Foundation" (hereinafter referred to as the "Foundation"), a non-profit association is established pursuant to Articles 60 et seq. of the Swiss Civil Code (ZGB).
1.2. The primary mission of the Foundation is the perpetual stewardship, research, development, and international propagation of the "Brahmastra" post-quantum resilient self-sovereign identity and offline decentralized mesh communication protocol.

## ARTICLE 2: FOUNDER SOVEREIGNTY AND PERPETUAL APPOINTMENT
2.1. The Lead Architect and Original Creator of the Brahmastra Protocol is designated as the **Founding Trustee & Sovereign Lead**.
2.2. The Founding Trustee holds an irrevocable, lifetime appointment to the Foundation Governance Council.
2.3. No resolution, organizational restructuring, dissolution, or appointment of board members shall be valid without the explicit, affirmative concurrence and written consent of the Founding Trustee.

## ARTICLE 3: EXCLUSIVE AUTHORITY OVER CORE CODE AND ARCHITECTURAL MODIFICATIONS
3.1. **Protocol Custody**: The technical stewardship of the Brahmastra codebase, cryptographic primitives, zero-knowledge circuits, and routing layer belongs exclusively under the supreme executive direction of the Founding Trustee.
3.2. **Modification Authority**: No pull requests, algorithmic alterations, protocol upgrades, hard forks, or consensus adjustments may be merged into the official canonical repository or designated as "Brahmastra Standard" without the cryptographic signature and explicit sign-off of the Founding Trustee.
3.3. **Veto Power**: The Founding Trustee retains absolute, non-appealable Veto Authority over all architectural decisions, commercial licensing proposals, and network upgrade milestones.

## ARTICLE 4: FINANCIAL TREASURY AND GRANT ALLOCATION SOVEREIGNTY
4.1. **Treasury Control**: All capital assets, research grants, donations, institutional disbursements, and cryptographic reserve treasuries held by the Foundation require the primary multi-signature authorization of the Founding Trustee.
4.2. **Budget Discretion**: No unilateral expenditure, legal retainer, operational budget, or third-party vendor allocation exceeding operational thresholds may be disbursed without the explicit sign-off of the Founding Trustee.

## ARTICLE 5: COUNCIL APPOINTMENT AND REMOVAL POWERS
5.1. The Founding Trustee reserves the sole discretionary authority to nominate, onboard, suspend, or terminate any member of the Advisory Council, Executive Board, or Technical Working Groups.
5.2. In the event of governance deadlocks or non-compliance with the founding mission, the decisive vote of the Founding Trustee shall supersede all collective votes of the Foundation Council.

## ARTICLE 6: EMERGENCY CIRCUIT-BREAKER AND ASSET MIGRATION
6.1. Under circumstances of hostile regulatory enforcement, external judicial overreach, or catastrophic cryptographic compromise, the Founding Trustee retains executive authority to trigger emergency pause mechanisms, fork protections, or re-domicile Foundation digital assets into a resilient jurisdiction.

## ARTICLE 7: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CANONICAL BRANDING
7.1. The official trademark, identity, cryptographic seeds, and architectural documentation of Brahmastra remain safeguarded under the perpetual governance established by the Founding Trustee.
7.2. Any party, contributor, or legal representative assisting the Foundation operates under delegated authority, which can be modified, audited, or revoked by the Founding Trustee at any point in accordance with the preservation of the protocol's core mission.

## ARTICLE 8: ADOPTION AND RATIFICATION
8.1. These Statutes are hereby ratified into the local repository framework on this 6th day of November, 2026, establishing the foundational governance charter for subsequent Swiss canton filing.
