# FCRA STATUTORY TREASURY MANDATE & INFLOW RULES
## BRAHMASTRA DHARMA TRUST (INDIA)

**Statutory Framework**: Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 (as amended in 2020)
**Oversight Authority**: Foreigners Division (FCRA Wing), Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India

1. **Designated Sole Remittance Route**:
   - All inbound foreign contributions routed exclusively through the designated State Bank of India (SBI), New Delhi Main Branch FCRA account.
   - Secondary institutional distribution routed strictly to certified zero-fee public welfare operational accounts.

2. **Statutory Administrative Expenditure Cap (20%)**:
   - Total operational, maintenance, legal, and audit expenditures strictly constrained below the 20% statutory ceiling mandated under the FCRA 2020 amendment.
   - 80% mandatory utilization dedicated to free oncology intervention, emergency medical infrastructure, and rural education.

3. **Sub-Granting Prohibition & Non-Speculative Lock**:
   - Total prohibition on sub-granting or onward transferring of foreign contributions to any other domestic trust, society, or non-profit entity.
   - Zero speculative investment, political activity, or private trustee benefit.

4. **Annual Reporting & On-Chain Audit**:
   - Mandatory annual Form FC-4 returns filing with audited dual-ledger verification reconciling Ministry of Home Affairs filings with certified public cryptographic rails.
