# STATUTORY COMPLIANCE REVIEW: INDEPENDENT BOARD STRUCTURE & CREATOR PREROGATIVE
## BRAHMASTRA SOVEREIGN FOUNDATION (ZUG, SWITZERLAND)

- **Statutory Framework**: Swiss Civil Code (Art. 83-89), Swiss Federal Supervisory Board for Foundations (ESA) Governance Guidelines
- **Core Findings**:
  1. Under Swiss Foundation Law, a foundation charter can lawfully separate institutional oversight from intellectual property stewardship and technological execution.
  2. Restricting the 5-7 independent board seats to legal defense, audit conformity, and endowment protection meets all statutory requirements of Swiss supervisory authorities.
  3. Vested creator architectural authority ensures non-profit purpose immutability without infringing upon regulatory oversight standards.
- **Audit Verdict**: Proposed board structure and sovereignty charter comply fully with Swiss foundation statutory jurisprudence.
