# Security Policy & White-Hat Bounty Guidelines

## 1. Responsible Disclosure & Safe Harbor
The Brahmastra Sovereign Core invites independent cryptographic researchers and ethical hackers to rigorously test our post-quantum mixnet and blind FHE compute infrastructure. Under our Safe Harbor terms, genuine research testing within defined scope will not incur legal retaliation.

## 2. In-Scope Targets
- Post-Quantum Lattice BFV Blind Evaluation logic
- Sphinx packet padding (4096-byte constant verification)
- 5-Node Mixnet timing resistance & zero-metadata guarantees

## 3. Rewards & Hall of Fame Recognition
Brahmastra currently operates under a **Reputation & Hall of Fame Model**. Verified security findings will be permanently acknowledged and credited in our official `docs/HALL_OF_FAME.md` ledger and repository release notes. Cash bounties remain deferred to future protocol grant distributions.
