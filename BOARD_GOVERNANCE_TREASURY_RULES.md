# BOARD OF DIRECTORS GOVERNANCE LIMITATIONS & TREASURY FIDUCIARY RULES
## ENTITY: BRAHMASTRA SOVEREIGN FOUNDATION (CANTON OF ZUG, SWITZERLAND)

**Supervisory Oversight**: Swiss Federal Supervisory Board for Foundations (ESA)
**Fiduciary Framework**: Tripartite Sovereign Treasury Safeguards

1. **Non-Custodial Directorship Constraint**:
   - The expanded 5-7 member independent Advisory Board possesses zero administrative signing keys on Gnosis Safe multi-sig treasury contracts.
   - Directors hold zero individual or unilateral withdrawal authority across foundation bank accounts, US T-Bills portfolios, or Swiss gold bullion vaults.

2. **Statutory Audit & Protective Oversight**:
   - Board powers are strictly confined to statutory compliance, independent balance sheet review, and cross-border legal defense.
   - Any financial sign-off required by the Board is limited to ratifying annual statutory audit statements prepared for Swiss regulatory authorities (ESA/FINMA).

3. **Inviolable Purpose Protection**:
   - The Board cannot alter, reallocate, or redirect capital reserved for core humanitarian objectives (emergency healthcare and pediatric surgeries).
   - Treasury capital preservation mandates (T-Bills rollover, allocated physical gold vaulting) remain legally binding and immune to board dissolution or hostile re-allocation.
