# BRAHMASTRA SOVEREIGN TREASURY & DISBURSEMENT POLICY

## 1. SOLE AUTHORIZATION PRINCIPLE
All financial reserves, treasury allocations, and grant disbursements require the explicit authorization and cryptographic signature of the Founding Architect.

## 2. MULTI-SIGNATURE SAFEGUARDS
Operational expenses exceeding base thresholds must adhere to multi-signature smart vaults where the Founder maintains master approval authority.

## 3. ASSET ALLOCATION INTEGRITY
Funds allocated for cryptographic research, network infrastructure, and protocol auditing shall not be redirected without Founder concurrence.
